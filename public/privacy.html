Driver camera privacy policy

Effective September 24, 2026. This policy describes the live camera feature in the company driver app.

What the camera feature does

On a company vehicle tablet, the driver app uses the camera for live company vehicle monitoring. After you select Agree and grant device permission, the camera connects automatically when you open the signed-in driver app. Authorized administrators in your company can view the live video.

What is collected and shared

The feature sends live camera video through the camera service. People and surroundings within the camera’s view may appear in the video. The camera session is associated with your driver account and company so that only authorized administrators in that company can access it.

The camera feature does not request microphone access and does not save audio or video recordings. It creates operational connection logs, including the account, company, time and whether access was requested for publishing or viewing. This policy does not cover separately submitted trip photos or documents.

When camera access is active

The camera is active while the driver app is open in the foreground. Camera video pauses when you switch away from the app and stops when you sign out or close it. Returning to the app reconnects or resumes the camera. Android’s camera-use indicator remains visible while the camera is active.

Your choice and device permission

Select Agree to accept this camera policy and continue to the device permission request. Select Decline to sign out without entering the driver app. Agreeing does not override Android camera permissions.

Camera access and a working camera connection are required to use this company driver app. There is no camera off switch on its dashboard. You can revoke camera permission in device settings or sign out; the driver app will require camera setup before you can use it again.

Remembering your agreement

The app remembers this policy version’s acceptance on this device for your company and driver account. Clearing app data, changing accounts, or an updated policy can require agreement again.

Questions

Contact your company administrator about vehicle camera placement, who is authorized to view video, operational log retention, workplace agreements, or privacy requests. This camera policy supplements your company’s applicable notices and agreements.
