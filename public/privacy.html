Company driver camera and recording policy

NEMT Solutions company tablets require camera permission and an agreement before the driver workspace opens. Selecting Decline signs you out.

What is captured

While the driver app is visible, the vehicle camera captures video without audio. The tablet’s operating-system camera indicator stays visible. The app stops capture when hidden or signed out. This build does not record in the background.

Who can view footage

Authorized administrators of your transport company can view live video and saved recordings. Other companies cannot access these recordings through the app.

Seven-day retention

Saved clips expire seven days after capture. Server cleanup automatically removes expired files on its next scheduled pass, normally within 15 minutes. A server outage can delay physical deletion; expired clips are unavailable through the app. Temporary playback links expire within five minutes and never extend the clip’s retention window.

Connection loss and tablet storage

During an internet interruption, video waits in private app storage on the company tablet and uploads after reconnection. Pending storage is limited to 200 MB; if it fills or recording fails, the driver must restore recording before continuing in the app. Unuploaded clips older than seven days are deleted when the queue next runs. An app force-close may lose the clip being captured.

Contact

Contact your company administrator about camera use, access to footage, or the company’s recording agreement. The owner sets the company support number in the company profile.
